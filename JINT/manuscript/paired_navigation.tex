\subsection{Field-observed obstacle passage on matched routes}\label{sec:paired-field}
The field protocol specified a goal 10~m ahead and a stationary box on the route. Success required observed passage beyond the box, a final axle midpoint within $0.50\m$ of the marked goal center, and no contact or manual intervention. The endpoint measure is the planar distance measured on the floor after the robot stopped. The comparison input in the field sheet is \JINTComparator{}. Each pair identifies two attempts assigned to the same start and obstacle placement. The tables count started attempts and retain incomplete pairs.
